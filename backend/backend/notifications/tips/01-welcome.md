QA-Board shows each commit's outputs and metrics next to a reference's, like the last release, on the same test
inputs every time. You see what a change did, down to the pixel, the percentile or the test, and share a link to what
you found.

Start with the docs for what you do:

- **Looking at results**: the [user guide](/docs/user-guide/overview), then
  [Your first review](/docs/user-guide/first-review), one commit reviewed in ten steps.
- **Adding a project**: [Project integration](/docs/introduction), from `qa init` to CI.
- **Running the server**: [Admin](/docs/deploy).
- **Seeing what other teams track**: [Use cases](/docs/use-cases), from image quality to latency, robotics and LLM
  agents.
- **Following what changes**: [What's new](app:/#whats-new), the sparkles button at the top of the sidebar.

QA-Board is open source, under the Apache 2.0 license. The code and the issues are on
[GitHub](https://github.com/Samsung/qaboard).

For the next few weeks, you get one short email a week, each with one thing to try in the web app. You can stop them anytime, from the link at the bottom.
